Growing tips worth keeping.

Water at the right time

Water early morning or evening to reduce evaporation loss and get more to the roots.

Let good bugs work for you

Ladybirds and other beneficial insects can control pests naturally — avoid killing them with broad sprays.

Check your soil

Healthy soil with organic matter supports healthier crops.

Rotate your crops

Changing where crops are planted can help reduce recurring pests and soil problems.

Mulch around your crops

Mulch helps keep soil moisture in and reduces competition from weeds.

Give plants enough space

Good spacing improves airflow and reduces conditions that encourage disease.

Watch the weather

Weather conditions can affect watering, spraying and disease risk.

Compost your organic waste

Compost can add organic matter and nutrients back into your soil.

Keep a simple field record

Recording planting, watering and spraying helps you learn what works.
