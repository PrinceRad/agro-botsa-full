Growing tips worth keeping.

Water at the right time

Water early morning or evening to reduce evaporation loss and get more to the roots.

Let good bugs work for you

Ladybirds and other beneficial insects can control pests naturally — avoid killing them with broad sprays.

Check your soil, not just your plants

Healthy, crumbly soil with visible organic matter usually means healthier crops above ground too.
